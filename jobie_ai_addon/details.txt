## Behaviour to know about
- **Alerts replace raw alerts:** with the add-on on, the unfiltered "every new job" Telegram alert is replaced by the filtered one. With it off, your old path runs unchanged.
- **Strict 0–2 filter:** "1–3 years" is excluded by default. Set `AI_MAX_REQUIRED_MAX_YEARS=3` to allow it.
- **Ambiguous jobs:** a job with no experience statement, or one that contradicts itself, goes to `review` and is never alerted. A phrase like "our company has 10 years of experience" can also push a job to `review`.
- **Resume PDFs fail closed:** they go only to chats named exactly `1 min`, `Fresher bot`, `5 min and 30 min ago`. If any of the three can't be mapped uniquely, no PDF is sent.
- **Control bot gets no PDF:** it sees the filename and SHA-256, because you said PDFs go only to the three chats. Approve sends the exact approved file.
- **Contacts depend on your accounts:** without a search API key and a Hunter key, you get no contacts. Nothing is made up, and free-tier quotas are capped in the code.
- **Render has no LaTeX:** the service that builds resumes needs `deploy/Dockerfile.addon`. The approval poller must run in exactly one process.
